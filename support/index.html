Earthquake Atlas support

Earthquake Atlas lets you explore recent earthquakes, aftershocks, historical seismicity, fault systems, and tsunami scenarios. It is a reference and education app. Follow official local alerts and instructions during an emergency.

  • Earthquake notifications. Open Now, then Alerts and sources. Turn on notifications and choose a magnitude, an area near you, or places to watch. Review iOS notification permissions if alerts are missing. For nearby alerts, allow location access.
  • Live Activities. Open an earthquake and tap Follow when available. Adjust automatic Live Activities in Alerts and sources.
  • Offline data. Open Now after reconnecting to refresh the cached feed. The app labels old data when it cannot retrieve a current feed.
  • Contact support. Email Earthquake Atlas support. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description. Leave out your exact location or other personal information.
